## Dates
- Submission period: 17 Nov 2026 11:00 PT to **12 Jan 2027 11:00 PT** (12 Jan 2027, 00:30 IST on 13 Jan).
- Judging: 27 Jan to 3 Feb 2027. Winners around 8 Feb 2027.
- Drafts can be saved on Devpost before the deadline; no edits after it.

## Eligibility (individual in India)
- Open to individuals at the age of majority where they live (18 in India), teams, and organisations.
- Excluded: residents of Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, any country comprehensively sanctioned by US OFAC, or anywhere local or US law forbids taking part. **India is not excluded.**
- Excluded: staff or agents of Cloud9, Devpost, JetBrains, GRID, judges and their families.
- No financial or preferential support from any of those entities.
- Solo entry allowed. More than one submission allowed if each is substantially different.

**Correction to the background:** a Category 3 entry competes for **two** cash prizes ($6,000, $2,000) plus the $1,000 bonus, not seven. The seven prizes are spread across three categories.

### Category 3 brief (exact points)
- Fast: a session ideally under 3 minutes, high replayability.
- Simple controls (mouse only, arrow keys, single button), understood with no tutorial; aim-trainer or reaction game named as examples.
- Booth-ready: resets cleanly between players, stays responsive in sustained use.
- Theme optional (Cloud9, JetBrains, VALORANT, LoL or other). Riot-inspired games must use original names, art, assets, presentation and follow Riot developer policies.
- Next level: trains a real competitive gamer skill; **local live leaderboard on a separate monitor, working offline without Wi-Fi** ("a massive plus").
- Judges must see a complete loop: start, play, clear end and score screen, and proof of clean reset and sustained responsiveness.

## Data and APIs
- Official GRID esports data (VALORANT, LoL) is available after registering and is optional for every category. Category 3 may use any data. We plan to use none, so no data licence questions arise.

## JetBrains requirement (real condition)
- Project must be built or significantly updated using at least one JetBrains tool: a JetBrains IDE (IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm, Rider, DataGrip), **JetBrains Air** (standalone app, IDE plugin, web or CLI) or **AI Coding Agent Junie**. Other editors may be used alongside.
- Stage One judging is pass/fail on whether the project "reasonably applies the required JetBrains tools".
- Technological Implementation criterion: "How thoroughly and skillfully does the project use JetBrains tools?"
- Evidence: text description must say which JetBrains tools were used and what each did; committing the `.idea`, `.junie` or `.air` folder is encouraged.
- Registrants get 1 All Products Pack code and 1 AI Ultimate code by email within 5 business days (while supplies last). AI Ultimate needs an IDE licence, All Products Pack, trial or EAP to activate. Student licences are not valid.
- Free route regardless of codes: WebStorm and other JetBrains IDEs have free non-commercial licences; trials exist too.

## Submission contents
- Public repo on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket with all source, assets and run instructions, and an open-source LICENSE at the root that shows in the repo About section.
- Working demo link if a website, else a judge-accessible build.
- Text description: features, and which JetBrains tools were used and what each did.
- Methodology write-up: approach and how it solves the category's problem.
- Demo video: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, footage of the project working on its target device, no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music without permission.
- Bonus: a direct URL to a write-up (e.g. `AI_SHOWCASE.md` in the repo) or short video about using JetBrains tools and AI.
- English. Free, unrestricted access for judges until judging ends.

## Judging
Stage One pass/fail (fits theme, applies JetBrains tools). Stage Two, equal weight: Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Quality of the Idea, Presentation. Ties broken in that order, so Technological Implementation (JetBrains use) is the first tiebreak. Judges may use AI-driven analysis and may judge only from text, images and video.

## GDC trip conditions
- Festival Pass to GDC 2027, San Francisco; round-trip economy flight and hotel (up to 5 nights) chosen by Sponsor, one person.
- Winner must name an airport, confirm they can attend, and **obtain any US visa themselves**. If they cannot attend (including visa refusal) the GDC part is forfeited; cash may still be paid.

## Payment and tax
- Paid after a winner affidavit and forms are returned within 10 business days; delivered within 60 days of receipt, by mail or bank transfer.
- Winner pays wiring and currency fees and all taxes in their home jurisdiction. Non-US residents may need a **W-8BEN**. Sponsor may withhold part for US tax law. Winner handles Indian foreign-exchange and reporting rules.
- Entering grants Sponsor, JetBrains and Devpost publicity rights to name, likeness and the submission for three years.
- Disputes: binding AAA arbitration, New York law.
